Currently, I am a doctoral reseacher at the UCLouvain Crypto Group, and I am very fortunate to be supervised by François-Xavier Standaert. I also work closely with Jean-Sébastien Coron from the University of Luxembourg.

My research is supported by the ERC Advanced Grant BRIDGE. I focus on the physical security of cryptographic algorithms, including:

  • Side-channel analysis for post-quantum cryptography
  • Design of more efficient masking gadgets
  • Exploiting algebraic structures for probing security

I obtained my Master degree from University of Luxembourg in 2025, under the supervision of Jean-Sébastien Coron. From 2023 to 2024, I worked as a student research assistant at SnT, where I closely collaborated with Hao Cheng, and Johann Großschädl. Prior to this, I earned my Bachelor degree in Information Security from Northwestern Polytechnical University, China, in 2022.
